# TBOP Unified Executive Permissions — Beta 2.5

## Full operational/admin-equivalent access

These roles all receive the same day-to-day club management access:

- President
- Vice President
- Secretary
- Treasurer
- Sergeant at Arms
- System Administrator

They can manage:
- Members and officer assignments
- Calendar/events
- Secretary workspace
- Treasurer workspace
- Voting/elections
- Repeater management
- Equipment
- News/content
- Analytics
- Approvals
- Backup/export
- Website/system settings
- Secure Document Vault

## Trustee

Trustees remain intentionally more limited and primarily read/review oriented.

## Repeater Trustee

Repeater Trustee receives:
- Repeater operations
- Repeater technical Vault area
- Equipment/inventory related to technical operations
- General member-level areas

Repeater Trustee does not automatically receive:
- Financial administration
- Member-role administration
- Executive-only system settings

## Technical Admin

`admin` remains a separate technical/recovery role for:
- emergency recovery
- permanent Vault deletion
- system-level maintenance

The five executive officers do NOT need to be changed to role `admin` to receive full operational access.
