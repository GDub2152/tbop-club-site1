# News & Announcements Guide

## Draft to Live
Officer Portal > News

Open a Draft and click **Publish Now**.

## Schedule
Choose a future date/time and click **Schedule**. It will remain hidden from
the public website until the selected time.

## Unpublish
Click **Unpublish** on a live post. It returns to Draft and disappears from
the public homepage.

## Archive
Click **Archive** to remove an old item while retaining it in Supabase.

## Pin
Pinned posts sort ahead of normal posts.

## Preview
Preview does not change the post's database status.
