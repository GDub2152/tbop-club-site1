In Memory Of

Jim Snell, W8DRZ

Silent Key • The Original W8DRZ

The Blowtorch of Parma Amateur Radio Club and the W8DRZ callsign are proudly carried forward in memory of Jim Snell, the original W8DRZ, whose lifelong passion for radio, broadcasting, and electronics left a lasting legacy.

Jim's fascination with radio began at an early age. As a child, he was known for taking things apart simply to understand how they worked—and, more often than not, putting them back together again. That curiosity soon grew into a passion for electronics, leading him to build his own radios in his bedroom and laying the foundation for a remarkable career.

As a young adult, Jim entered the world of broadcasting at WBNO in Bryan, Ohio, where he hosted a late-night radio program known as “Shadows of Knight with Jim Knight.” His talent and enthusiasm eventually took him to the Richfield Coliseum, where he worked high above the arena in the broadcast center, helping cover games, concerts, and major events.

In 1978, Jim realized his lifelong dream when he joined Channel 8 in Cleveland as a Broadcast Engineer. He dedicated the next 35 years to keeping one of Cleveland's premier television stations on the air before retiring in 2013.

Away from television, Jim remained devoted to amateur radio, proudly operating under the callsign W8DRZ. Ham radio was more than a hobby—it reflected the same curiosity, technical skill, and love of communication that defined his life.

Today, The Blowtorch of Parma Amateur Radio Club proudly honors Jim's memory by carrying forward the W8DRZ legacy. We strive to inspire new amateur radio operators, encourage technical learning, support public service, and share the same passion for amateur radio that Jim exemplified throughout his life.

His legacy remains part of every signal we send. 73, JIM • W8DRZ
